"Write in New Ways"

This is an erasure and continuation poem of William Aarnes’s “Write”. An erasure poem is where you take a piece of text media and remove certain words to make a poem or a new one if you are using an existing poem.

"Write" by William Aarnes (Original)

Write like the water
rising in the reservoir inlet
during a week of steady rainfall,
the boardwalk past ankle-deep.

Write like the car
still idling down the street,
the driver keeping herself warm,
unable to make up her mind.

Write like the tornado
leveling the living room
and filling the window well
with recognizable debris.

Write like the elevator
opening its door to no one
and then waiting long enough
for that passenger to get in.

Erasure and continuation poem by TrevorJay Andrews

Write like water
rising the reservoir
week of steady rainfall,
boardwalk ankle-deep.

Write like fire
blazing through streets,
filling the views
with hope and fear.

Write like rain
tapping the roof
steady and loud
to drown everything.

Like the car
idling down the street,
driver keeping warm,
unable to make her mind.

Like the bridge
spanning the river,
its arch high,
current rushes underneath.

Like the foot
walking the street,
carrying weight forward,
knowing where to go.

Write the tornado
leveling the living
filling the window
with debris.

Write the chisel
striking rough marble,
chipping away excess
the shape is free.

Write the earth
sturdy and stable
pride and strength
under the ground.

Write like the elevator
opening to no one
and then waiting long
for that passenger to get in.

Write like the stairs
rising by floor,
waiting for no one
to make the climb.

Write like the door
of the hall,
open enough
to let light through.
