Ekphrastic Poems

An ekphrastic poem is a poem inspired by a painting or another piece of media. For example one might look at a painting and decided to describe how it looks in the poem, how it makes them feel, or both. They can even make their own stories for the poems. Below is my example with the Starry Night by Van Gogh.

The Howling town at night.

Oh the violet blue skyline.
The lone tree outlooking the town,
Burning holes in the sky.
How I used to stare up
Under the tree as I cried.
The town that shut me out,
Why do they shout?
Is it due to the patterns in the stars?
Due to the moon and earth go round the sun?
