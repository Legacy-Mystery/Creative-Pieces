A guided poem is one made by answering different prompts. These can be regarding childhood memories, your current surroundings, what you're feeling in the moment, or just random things. You can list your answers in order of the questions, or however you wish. You can make them all rhyme, some rhyme, or none. Below is an example I did using these questions:

Guided Poem by TrevorJay Andrews

  1. Having every Barbie movie.
  2. A dragon colored like a ruby.
  3. I see pale trees of five.
  4. Looking outside I feel alive.
  5. My old house was the color of a grey bunny.
  6. It is very warm and sunny.
  7. Today I wish to start a short story.
  8. In a year I want my books to achieve glory.

Writer's Reflection

For this piece, I chose to connect the prompt answers using rhyming couplets (AABBCCDD) so the individual thoughts flow together like a single story—moving from childhood nostalgia and fantasy imagery, out to my immediate surroundings, and ending on my goals as an author.
